# Blockiva Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 8 September 2026

Blockiva is an offline-first block puzzle game. You can play without creating an account, signing in, or providing your name, phone number, or email address.

## Information stored on your device

Blockiva stores game information locally on your device so your progress can continue between sessions. This may include your best score, active game state, coins, streak/progression data, unlocked themes, achievements, sound preference, and similar gameplay settings.

This local game data is not uploaded to a Blockiva-owned server. Removing the app or clearing its app storage may remove this locally stored information.

## Advertising

Blockiva uses Google Mobile Ads to provide advertising features such as optional rewarded ads. For example, a player may choose to watch a rewarded ad in exchange for an in-game benefit such as continuing a run.

Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device identifiers, IP address, app interactions, advertising data, diagnostics, and other information required to deliver, measure, secure, and personalize or limit advertising in accordance with applicable law and the user's consent choices.

Where required, Blockiva uses Google's consent tools before requesting ads. Advertising availability may vary by region, device, network connection, and consent status.

Google's privacy information is available at: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Permissions and network access

Blockiva's core puzzle gameplay works without a Blockiva account or backend. Internet access is used for third-party advertising and related consent/privacy services when those features are available.

## Children's privacy

Blockiva is intended for a general audience and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information directly from children through a Blockiva account system. Parents and guardians should review device and advertising privacy settings as appropriate.

## Data control and deletion

Because Blockiva stores gameplay progress locally, you can remove that local data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. Data processed independently by Google or other advertising providers is governed by their own privacy controls and policies.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Blockiva's features, advertising configuration, legal requirements, or third-party services change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.
