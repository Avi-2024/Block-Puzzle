## Long description

Blockiva is a satisfying block puzzle built for quick, relaxing play sessions.

Drag colorful block pieces onto an 8×8 board, complete full rows or columns, trigger clears, build combos, and keep the board open for your next set of pieces. Every move matters: plan ahead, protect useful space, and push for a new best score.

### Why play Blockiva?

- Simple drag-and-place controls
- Fast 8×8 block puzzle gameplay
- Colorful glossy blocks and satisfying clear effects
- Score, best score, combos, streaks, coins, themes and achievements
- Offline-first core gameplay
- No account or login required
- Game progress stored locally on your device
- Optional rewarded ads for selected in-game benefits when available
- Sound and haptic feedback designed for short casual sessions

Blockiva is easy to understand but rewards smart planning. Use all three pieces carefully, make room before the board fills up, and keep your combo going for bigger scores.

Play at your own pace, return to your current run later, and keep improving your personal best.
